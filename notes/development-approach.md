# Development approach

## Turn an idea into a testable loop

I start by describing the player's first action, the result they can understand immediately and the reason they would choose to do it again. Features that do not strengthen that loop wait until the prototype provides evidence for them.

For tools, the same principle applies: one clear task, visible progress and a useful result before adding more panels or settings.

## Separate design intent from implementation evidence

A concept is a hypothesis. A prototype demonstrates an interaction. A passing test verifies the behavior it actually exercises. Player retention and commercial performance require real measurements; they cannot be inferred from a polished mockup.

## Use AI with clear ownership of decisions

AI helps draft code, explore alternatives, summarize documentation and identify edge cases. I provide the product brief and constraints, compare the results against the intended behavior and iterate on the experience.

The portfolio identifies AI-assisted work rather than presenting every implementation detail as unaided authorship. Generated code still needs verification, and generated research still needs sources.

## Prefer small, inspectable systems

The intended game-development approach is modular, server-authoritative and conservative about persistence and purchases. Complex asset pipelines, bespoke physics and unnecessary integrations are scope decisions, not automatic signs of quality.

This describes the approach I want future Roblox prototypes to demonstrate. It is not a claim that a commercial game using those systems has already shipped.
